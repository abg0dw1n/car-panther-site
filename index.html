About the Panthers

Founded in 1995 as the NFL's 29th franchise, the Carolina Panthers represent North and South Carolina. Playing at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, NC, the franchise is built on relentless defense, blue-collar pride, and the enduring "Keep Pounding" mantra.

Franchise Milestones & Legacy

  • 1995 Inaugural Season: Set the then-record for most wins by an expansion team (7–9) under Coach Dom Capers.
  • 2003 "Cardiac Cats" (Super Bowl XXXVIII): Won the NFC Championship behind Jake Delhomme, Stephen Davis, and Steve Smith Sr., making their first Super Bowl appearance.
  • 2015 NFC Champions (Super Bowl 50): Historic 15–1 regular season led by MVP Cam Newton, Luke Kuechly, and Coach Ron Rivera.
  • The "Keep Pounding" Tradition: Inspired by late linebacker and coach Sam Mills during his battle with cancer in 2003; now symbolized by the giant Keep Pounding Drum banged before every home game.

Hall of Honor Legends

Honoring the bedrock figures who defined Carolina Football:

Sam Mills (#51)

Linebacker / Coach. The heart and soul of the franchise and originator of "Keep Pounding." Pro Football Hall of Famer.

Steve Smith Sr. (#89)

Wide Receiver. Triple Crown winner (2005) and competitive fire behind the Panthers' greatest air attacks.

Julius Peppers (#90)

Defensive End. N.C. native, 2024 Pro Football Hall of Fame inductee, and one of the most dominant edge rushers in NFL history.

Jake Delhomme (#17)

Quarterback. Led the legendary 2003 "Cardiac Cats" run to Super Bowl XXXVIII with clutch postseason comebacks.
