⏱ HISTORIC FRANCHISE MILESTONES

1995 - PRESENT

95 1995: Inaugural Season

Founded as the NFL's 29th franchise. Dom Capers led Carolina to a 7-9 inaugural record—the most wins by an expansion team in NFL history at the time.

03 2003: "Cardiac Cats" & Super Bowl XXXVIII

Behind Jake Delhomme, Stephen Davis, and Steve Smith Sr., the Panthers executed thrilling comeback victories and won the NFC Championship, then fell to the New England Patriots 32-29 in Super Bowl XXXVIII.

15 2015: 15-1 Season & MVP Cam Newton

Historic 15-1 regular season led by League MVP Cam Newton and Luke Kuechly (Pro Football Hall of Fame, Class of 2026), securing Carolina's second NFC Title. The Panthers lost Super Bowl 50 to the Denver Broncos, 24-10.

25 2025: NFC South Champions

In Dave Canales' second season, Carolina won the NFC South at 8-9 and returned to the playoffs, falling to the Rams in the wild-card round.

BANK OF AMERICA STADIUM FAN GUIDE

Essential game day details for Uptown Charlotte:

🏟️ BANK OF AMERICA STADIUM

CHARLOTTE, NC • CAPACITY: 75,037

KEEP POUNDING FIELD 🏈

🏟️ Location

800 S Mint St, Charlotte, NC 28202. Uptown Charlotte.

🥁 Drum Tradition

A guest drummer strikes the Keep Pounding drum just before kickoff of every home game, a tradition born from Sam Mills' 2003 "Keep Pounding" speech.

🍔 Tailgating

Fans arrange and pre-pay their own parking (30,000+ spaces within a 10-15 minute walk). Keep grills 25 ft from buildings and 10 ft from vehicles, never leave them unattended, and note the Mint Street Lot doesn't allow alcohol or grilling.

🎒 Clear Bag Policy

NFL Clear Bag Policy enforced: clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12", or a one-gallon clear freezer bag. A small clutch (up to 4.5" x 6.5") is also allowed.

🏗️ Renovation Coming

A $1.3 billion stadium renovation is planned. Only behind-the-scenes work happens during the 2026 season, fan-facing construction begins in 2027, and completion is targeted for 2030. The stadium stays open for games, and the final capacity hasn't been set.
