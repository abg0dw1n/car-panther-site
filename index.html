1995: Inaugural Season & Dominance

Founded as the NFL's 29th franchise. Dom Capers led Carolina to a 7-9 inaugural record—the most wins by an expansion team in NFL history at the time.

2003: "Cardiac Cats" & Super Bowl XXXVIII

Behind Jake Delhomme, Stephen Davis, and Steve Smith Sr., the Panthers executed thrilling comeback victories, capturing the NFC Championship and advancing to Super Bowl XXXVIII.

2015: 15-1 Season & MVP Cam Newton

A historic 15-1 regular season culminating in MVP honors for Cam Newton, Defensive Player of the Year honors for Luke Kuechly, and an NFC Championship title (Super Bowl 50).

The "Keep Pounding" Legacy

Coined by Hall of Famer Sam Mills during his courageous fight against cancer in 2003. "Keep Pounding" remains the eternal rallying cry of the Carolinas.
