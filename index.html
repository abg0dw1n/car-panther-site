⏱ HISTORIC FRANCHISE MILESTONES

1995 - PRESENT

95 1995: Inaugural Season & Dominance

Founded as the NFL's 29th franchise. Dom Capers led Carolina to a 7-9 inaugural record—the most wins by an expansion team in NFL history at the time.

03 2003: "Cardiac Cats" & Super Bowl XXXVIII

Behind Jake Delhomme, Stephen Davis, and Steve Smith Sr., the Panthers executed thrilling comeback victories, capturing the NFC Championship and advancing to Super Bowl XXXVIII.

15 2015: 15-1 Season & MVP Cam Newton

Historic 15-1 regular season led by League MVP Cam Newton and Defensive Player of the Year candidate Luke Kuechly, securing Carolina's second NFC Title.
