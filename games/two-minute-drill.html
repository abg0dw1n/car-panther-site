🐾 Panthers Arcade

Two-Minute Drill

You’re Bryce Young. The clock is running down and the Panthers are behind. Call the plays, manage the clock, and win it.

Difficulty
  • The defense picks a call every snap. Some plays beat certain defenses: quick passes beat the blitz, runs and short passes eat up a soft prevent defense.
  • Incomplete passes and going out of bounds stop the clock. Runs and catches in the field keep it running, and you lose about 15 seconds getting to the next snap.
  • Use timeouts and the spike to save time. Turnovers, a failed 4th down or running out of time end the drive.
